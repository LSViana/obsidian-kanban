# 2.2.0

Changes since 2.1.0.

## Child cards
- Click a parent card's "n/m done" badge to see a list of its children, grouped by lane, plus archived ones. Pick a child to jump to it. When card checkboxes are shown, you can check children off from the list.
- "Add child card" now opens with room to type and the cursor ready. If you leave it empty or press Escape, the new card is removed.

## New notes from cards
- New "Move card body to new note" option (global and per board, off by default). When on, "New note from card" moves the text below the card's first line (when a blank line separates them) into the new note, and the card keeps its title as a link to the note.

## Selected cards
- Press Del (or Backspace on macOS) to delete the selected cards. It asks first when more than one card is selected.
- Hold Alt (Option on macOS) while dragging to copy cards instead of moving them. Copied parent and child cards stay linked.

## Menus
- Delete actions in menus are shown in red, like in Obsidian's own menus.
